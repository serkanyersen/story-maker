# Story Maker App

Story maker is a small web app, that will help parents create small bed time stories with their kids using AI. it's a fun way to let parents and kids to bond, help encourage more story times and reading, as well as foster imagination in kids.

Parents can chose two modes to create stories

## Quick Mode

in this mode, options are limited to only the essentials, parent will be given a simple form to generate a story. questions can be as follows

- Age Group
  - 2-4
  - 4-6
  - 6-8
  - 8-10
- Duration
  - Short (2-5 min)
  - Medium (5-10 min)
  - Full
- Theme
  - Let AI Decide
  - Urban
  - Country / Farm
  - Fantasy
  - Fairly Tale
  - Jungle Adventure
  - Pirates
  - Science Fiction (space)
  - more...
    ...
  - Custom
- Hero
  - Boy Child (5-10)
  - Girl Child (5-10)
  - Young Male (11-15)
  - Young Female (11-15)
- Companions
  - None
  - Boy Child (5-10)
  - Girl Child (5-10)
  - Young Male (11-15)
  - Young Female (11-15)
  - Male Parent
  - Female Parent
  - Any Father Figure
  - Any Mother Figure
- Journey
  - Little Hardship but challenges overcome
  - No Hardship, but lessons learned
  - No Hardship, No lessons, just a fun adventure story
  - Curiosity, Investigations, Victory
  - Silly fun time
- Ending
  - Happy Ending
  - Thought Provoking Ending
  - Hopeful Ending
  - Big laughter

After they make the selections, arguments are turned into a set of instructions for AI to generate a story. We tell AI to generate the story in pages, with images for each. we can then take those and render accordingly.

## Advanced Mode

This mode is mostly the same, however, you can additionally create characters with background and save them. That way, you can select these characters and add them to stories. Advanced mode also remembers your previous stories and let you expand on them.
